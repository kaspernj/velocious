`configuration.awaitPendingBroadcasts()` now also drains local (per-process) websocket channel subscriber deliveries that were in flight when the barrier was called, restoring the snapshot-barrier guarantee that no broadcast work outlives the call. Previously only the host/worker publish queues were awaited, so a slow subscriber delivery launched fire-and-forget could keep owning database connections past the barrier and into the next test file, exhausting the connection pool (CI showed `Pool max=10; inUse=9` with eight `Frontend model websocket event tenant` holders before `spec/database/record/attachments-spec.js`). Individual delivery failures stay isolated and logged; work enqueued after the call is still not awaited; the fire-and-forget broadcast API is unchanged.
