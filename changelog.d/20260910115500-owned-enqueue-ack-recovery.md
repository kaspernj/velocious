Recover a generation-owned background-job enqueue once after an ambiguous post-send acknowledgement failure, whether the connection is lost or its deadline expires, by replaying its exact existing producer proof and invocation identity only after the main accepts the generation fence. The durable store returns the original job instead of inserting a duplicate; legacy/default connections, pre-send generation failures, explicit enqueue rejections, and ordinary enqueues retain their existing fail-loud behavior and `idempotencyKey` semantics.
