# Background jobs: pooled-runner IPC-silence watchdog

A pooled runner whose socketduct transport wedges silently keeps its IPC channel open but stops running its own timers, so the worker's disconnect/exit handlers never fire and its in-flight jobs stay stranded until a human kills the process. The worker now watches every pooled child that owns in-flight jobs: any IPC message (readiness, acceptance, memory observation, shutdown observation, or job outcome) re-arms a per-child silence timer, since a healthy child samples itself every 10 seconds while jobs run. Silence past `pooledRunnerSilenceTimeoutMs` (default 120000ms, env `VELOCIOUS_BACKGROUND_JOBS_POOLED_RUNNER_SILENCE_TIMEOUT_MS`, `0` disables) declares the child wedged: the worker requests shutdown under the new `child_silence` reason, reports the whole in-flight set as failed through the established failure path so the jobs requeue and capacity is re-advertised, signals the wedged process (SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after the existing grace), and logs a compact `pooled-child-silence` JSON line naming the child for post-incident attribution.
