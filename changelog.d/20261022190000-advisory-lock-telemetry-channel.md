Advisory-lock duration telemetry now emits on `configuration.getTelemetryEvents()` as `telemetry` instead of `framework-error`/`all-error`. These reports are measurements, not failures: delivering them on the error channel made every contended lock look like a bug, so bug reporters subscribed to `framework-error` filed one report, one symbolication job, and one notification per event. Consumers that routed them to a bug reporter should subscribe to the telemetry channel and send them to metrics/logs instead.
