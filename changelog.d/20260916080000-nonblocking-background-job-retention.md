Terminal-job retention no longer blocks enqueues: `pruneTerminalJobs` selects bounded candidate pages outside the singleton count-revision mutation lock (deterministic ordering by terminal timestamp, then id), and only the short serialized transaction revalidates the selected ids, deletes the survivors, and publishes the count delta from the database's actual affected-row count — a concurrent pruner removing candidates first no longer makes a pass declare convergence early. Readiness additionally ensures three additive composite indexes on `background_jobs` — `index_background_jobs_completed_retention (status, completed_at_ms, id)`, `index_background_jobs_failed_retention (status, failed_at_ms, id)`, and `index_background_jobs_orphaned_retention (status, orphaned_at_ms, id)` — so retention discovery scales with the expired range instead of scanning every row of a terminal status.
